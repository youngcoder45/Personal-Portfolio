Before Niri, I lived inside **KDE Plasma**. These dotfiles are the polished
product of that era: custom KWin rules, a Neovim setup I still pinch parts from,
Fish configs, Kitty, and a Fastfetch that makes the login screen jealous.

This repo is my "productivity workflow reference" — the serious half of the
twins (the playful half is the Niri minimal rice).

```lua
-- neovim: the part I kept after leaving the taskbar walled garden
vim.g.mapleader = ' '
vim.keymap.set('n', '<C-s>', ':w<CR>', { silent = true })
```

## Highlights

- KWin scripting for window behaviour that Plasma's GUI can't express.
- `nvim/` — a hand-tuned LSP config, telescope-centric, sprite-free.
- `fish/` and `kitty/` — the shell and terminal I keep coming back to.
- Fastfetch config that prints the bird and the brain.
